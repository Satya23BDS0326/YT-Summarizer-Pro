How RAG (Retrieval-Augmented Generation) works here:
Transcript is extracted from the YouTube video (Telugu, Hindi, English — any language).
Text is split into 400-word overlapping chunks and embedded into 384-dimensional vectors using sentence-transformers.
All vectors are stored in a FAISS index for fast cosine similarity search.
When you ask a question, it's embedded and the top 4 matching chunks are retrieved.
Retrieved chunks + your question are sent to Groq LLaMA-3.3-70b for a precise, grounded answer.
